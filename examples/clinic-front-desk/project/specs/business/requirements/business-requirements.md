# Business requirements

> Drafted by ProdOps from the brief, then edited by hand.

- The receptionist can register a patient in under a minute, so the person at the desk is not kept waiting.
- The receptionist can book a visit knowing it will not double-book a therapist or a patient.
- A therapist can write and sign a short note for a visit while the patient is still in the room.
- The clinic owner can see the day at a glance: who is booked, who has arrived, who has not confirmed and who did not come.
- No-shows fall because every booking is confirmed before the day and unconfirmed bookings are visible on the front desk.
