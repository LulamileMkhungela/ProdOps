# Design requirements

> Drafted by ProdOps from the brief, then edited by hand.

- Front desk: counts and the day's diary on one screen; the next action for each visit is one tap away.
- Patients: a registration form beside a searchable list; on a phone the form comes first, then the list.
- Appointments: a booking form beside the list of bookings, with the reason shown next to the field when a booking is refused.
- Visit notes: one card per patient who has arrived, with Save draft and Sign note.
- Every screen is usable at 375px width with no sideways scrolling.
- Every control can be reached and used with the keyboard; the focus outline is always visible.
- Status is shown with a word as well as a colour.
- Buttons and fields are at least 44px tall.
- Text meets a contrast ratio of 4.5 to 1 against its background.
