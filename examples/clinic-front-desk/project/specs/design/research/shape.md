## What we think we know
- No-shows go unmanaged: Appointments are booked but not confirmed, and empty slots are never backfilled.
- Notes are written after hours: Clinical documentation competes with the next patient, so it is either rushed or unpaid overtime.
- Billing lags the visit: Codes and claims are reconstructed days later from memory, and revenue quietly leaks.

## What we need to learn
- Does “Phones ring at the front desk while the queue grows” match what practice managers actually experience?
- Does “Templates do not match how she actually writes” match what clinicians actually experience?

## Touchpoints
- First run to first value — A new account lands with no records of its own.
- Daily core loop — A shift starts and the queue is already busy.
- Exception and recovery — A status stops moving for longer than its own threshold.
- Set up the workspace — Trial started, no integrations connected.

## Plan
- 3–5 short interviews per persona
- One usability pass on the first journey
