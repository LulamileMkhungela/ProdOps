# Standards

> **Local template draft.** Generated in your browser from the brief by the ProdOps compiler. No AI model was used and nothing was validated with real users. Replace anything that does not fit before approval.

- The patient is in the room, be brief
- One record, one truth
- Never ask twice for the same detail
- The system should be legible to someone who did not build it.
- Destructive actions require a typed confirmation, not a modal click.
- Accessibility: contrast at least 4.5:1, keyboard reachable, labelled inputs
- Write requirements as testable statements
- Mark assumptions as assumptions
